R — profit in units of what the trade risked. The stop distance is one R, so a clean stop-out lands near −1.00 and anything past −1.5 means the stop is not working.
net exp — dollars of one-way market exposure left after the hedge, (1−β)/(1+|β|) of gross. Zero only at β=1. At β<0 both legs point the same way and net exceeds either leg.
→ stop — right of centre is distance travelled toward the stop; left is distance toward take-profit. The two halves are scaled separately because they are not the same distance away.
