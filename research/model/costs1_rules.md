# COSTS1 decision rules (written before any L2 file is pulled or any cost is computed)

Task (planner, from the user): does the edge survive realistic execution costs? Analysis on the saved vault and test-month trades. No tuning of q, stops or timeout. Costs are reported, not optimised around.
Runs in scope (priority): q 1% / no stop / maker and taker, max_open 10 and 20, vault tags `vault_open10` / `vault_open20` and test months `base` / `open20`. The other 44 saved runs per side are NOT re-costed unless the planner asks (the L2 pull covers only the priority trades).

## 0. Conventions taken from the engine (s1_profit1.py, verified by reading it)
- Entry fill: wall-clock minute `t0` of the trade file; priced at the close of bar label `t0 - 1` (reference price `ref_in`). Exit fill: wall-clock `exit_time`, priced at the close of bar label `exit_time - 1` (`ref_out`).
- Legs: `leg_a = notional / (1 + |beta|)`, `leg_b = notional * |beta| / (1 + |beta|)`; `dir_a = -1 if z >= 0 else +1`, `dir_b = (1 if z >= 0 else -1) * (1 if beta >= 0 else -1)`; dir +1 = buy at entry, sell at exit. Exit leg USD = entry leg USD x exp(dlog price from entry to exit) from the cleaned 1-minute closes.
- Engine fees: every non-liquidation exit, including stops and timeouts, is charged the run's own fee mode (maker runs: 0.015%), entry too. Slippage is 0 in every saved run. Funding as saved.
- Zero-cost reference per trade: the saved `net` (gross - fees - funding, slippage 0).

## 1. Books
- Entry book for a leg = the snapshot with the minimum exchange time inside UTC minute `t0` (extractor build9_l2_books.py); exit book = same for minute `exit_time`. Books at the minutes after (up to +15) serve the waiting windows and the taker fallback.
- If the wanted minute has no snapshot in the pulled files: use the nearest wanted minute within +-1; else the "no book" proxy (below). The count of trade-legs by source (own minute / +-1 / proxy) is printed per period and tier.
- Beyond-book: if the 20 levels hold less USD than the leg, the stored levels are walked at their prices and only the REMAINDER is priced at the last stored level (an ESTIMATE (not a bound in either direction, checker 2026-11-14)) and the leg is flagged `beyond_book`; (RESOLUTION 2026-11-14, planner: sections 1 and 8 disagreed; section 1's wording is the HEADLINE, with the table the "stored levels" are the CURVE points: the first D_last USD at the curve's average cost, the remainder at the last stored level. Section 8's wording "the leg is priced at book_reach_bp", i.e. the WHOLE leg at the last level, is printed beside it, with the net without beyond-book trades, for both periods. The vault letter is unaffected; test-month numbers change and both versions are shown.) the share of legs flagged is printed, and totals are also printed with those trades excluded.
- No-book proxy (labelled, weak): half the ctxs impact spread, 0.5 x (impact_ask - impact_bid) / mid, per leg, from the saved ctxs cache at that minute. It is measured at the exchange's fixed impact notional, not at our size, so it overstates the cost of small legs; it is a ceiling-like proxy, not a measurement.

## 2. Taker fill (entry and exit)
- Cost = walk the opposite side of the book with the leg's USD (price x size, cumulative); average price `p_fill`. Buy: cost_bp = (p_fill / mid - 1) x 1e4; sell: (1 - p_fill / mid) x 1e4, `mid` = (best bid + best ask) / 2 of that book. Taker fee 0.045% on the leg USD.
- Exact accounting against the engine's reference price, per leg: entry gain = dir x leg_usd x (ref_in - p_fill_in) / ref_in; exit gain = dir x leg_usd_out x (p_fill_out - ref_out) / ref_out. net_new = saved gross + sum of gains - new fees - saved funding. The decomposition vs book mid (half spread, walk) is printed next to it; (ref - mid) should average about zero (printed as a check).

## 3. Maker fill (maker runs only; both legs attempt maker; taker runs are all-taker)
- Entry limit: buy leg at the best bid, sell leg at the best ask of the entry book. Waiting window W minutes starting at `t0` (bars labelled t0 .. t0 + W - 1 from the build2 1-minute cache). Primary W = 5; sensitivity W = 1 and 15.
- LENIENT fill: first bar with low <= limit (buy) / high >= limit (sell). STRICT fill: first bar with low <= limit - tick (buy) / high >= limit + tick (sell), where tick = the smallest positive price step between adjacent levels in the entry book (an estimate; labelled). The two are the range; no queue position is observed (no tick data), so no point estimate is claimed between them.
- Fill price = the limit; fee maker 0.015%. A leg filled at bar f has fill time f (minute start; sub-minute order unknown).
- Both legs filled within W -> trade kept (entered at f_late; the exit is the saved exit, so a leg filled at f >= exit minute makes the trade MISSED). Exactly one leg unfilled at the end of W -> that leg is crossed as taker at the book of minute t0 + W (walk as in 2; the drift to that minute is inside the accounting vs ref_in), the trade is kept. Both legs unfilled -> trade MISSED (net 0, no cost; the slot it occupied in the saved run is not refilled).
- Exit, exit reason mu in a maker run: limit at the best passive price of the exit book, window W from `exit_time` with the same lenient / strict test on bars exit_time .. exit_time + W - 1; unfilled at W -> taker at the book of minute exit_time + W (price drift accounted vs ref_out). Exit reason stop or timeout, and every exit in taker runs: taker at the exit-minute book. The saved maker-run exits for stops/timeouts used the maker fee: its effect is reported as its own line (net of the existing maker numbers with those exits' fees raised to taker, nothing else changed; no run is edited).
- Fill rate = share of legs and share of trades filled, overall, by period and by liquidity tier. Adverse selection: for missed vs filled trades (each rule) compare the saved labels `reach_10h`, `minutes_to_mu`, and the saved gross in sigma (means, n, standard errors; no test statistics beyond that).

## 4. Two-leg risk
- For trades where the legs fill at different bars: gap in minutes, share of trades, and the unhedged leg's log-price move (bp, signed to our position) between its fill and the other leg's fill (or W), from the cleaned closes; distribution (mean, p50, p90, worst) and its total PnL effect (already inside the net via the exact accounting).

## 5. Re-costed results
- Per run and period: trades filled, net PnL, profit factor (sum of positive nets / sum of negative nets), Sharpe (daily realised PnL by exit day, mean / std x sqrt(365)), max drawdown (daily realised equity from $1,000, sizing held as saved), shown next to the SAME method on the zero-cost saved trades and next to the engine's own reported numbers. Equity is rebuilt from per-trade nets in exit-time order; compounding/sizing and slot refilling are NOT re-fed (stated).
- Same by coin (a trade counts for both legs, as in Part C), by pair, and by liquidity tier (terciles of each coin's median daily traded notional, build2 1-minute cache, as Part C); a trade takes its less liquid leg's tier. Question to answer from it: is the least liquid third's share of net (82.9% vault maker open10) real after costs, or flattering?
- Break-even uniform extra slippage per fill under this model vs the arithmetic of Part C (vault q 1% no stop maker open10: 5.43 bp; taker 1.77 bp).
- Sensitivity as a RANGE: W (1, 5, 15) x fill rule (lenient, strict) x impact multiplier on the walk and proxy cost (0.5, 1, 2). The impact term is an estimate, labelled.

## 6. Yardsticks and questions
- (a) Volume vs depth: fit walk cost (net of half-spread) on sqrt(leg / minute traded notional) over legs with a book; report slope, R^2 and the rank correlation, by tier; the fitted line is used only as a labelled estimate where a book has no depth for the leg (beyond_book), never to overwrite a measured cost.
- (b) Leverage caps: trades and net by leverage value (share on 3x, 5x, 10x...); what changes if a cap differs is stated as sizing (notional) not signals; historical caps are not available and nothing is simulated.
- Entry delay (1, 5, 15 min): write-up only, no engine change, no run.

## 7. Headline, answer rule and reporting conventions (checker review 2026-11-13, R1-R6, fixed before the analysis script exists)
- R1. HEADLINE CELL, fixed now: maker runs = strict fill rule, W = 5, impact multiplier 1, beyond-book legs at the last level (lower bound); taker runs = impact multiplier 1. The answer sentence is generated by the script from the sensitivity grid: "net positive in k of N sensitivity cells; headline net X (vault) and Y (test months)", per configuration, with no pass/fail wording. A day-block bootstrap interval on the headline net (as in S1-D) is suggested to the planner; it is not added without their call.
- R2. A bar that is missing or NaN inside a fill window counts as no fill, and the number of such legs is printed.
- R3. Beyond-book legs: the HEADLINE uses the last stored level (section 1). The fitted line of 6a is shown only as a separate, labelled column; it never enters the headline.
- R4. Profit factor, Sharpe and drawdown are from daily REALISED PnL by exit day. The engine's own are from marked account value; they are shown as reference only. The like-for-like baseline is the same method on the zero-cost saved trades.
- R5. The first snapshot of a minute is about 0.3 s in (pilot, early periods) and about 2.7 s in (2026-09-15) while the bar low/high covers the whole minute, so a lenient fill in the first bar can use trades from before the order existed: lenient is an upper bound on the fill rate. Print snap_off_s by period.
- R6. The headline table carries the missed-trade share next to net per FILLED trade, and net per trade of the SAVED set (missed counted as 0), so a rising net per filled trade cannot hide a falling fill rate. Missed shares are printed by cause (both legs unfilled; a leg filled at or after the exit minute) per period and run.
- Print (ref - mid) by liquidity tier (the engine's reference is a last-trade close and carries bid-ask bounce).
- Keep-and-cross at the end of W is the primary one-leg rule. Crossing the second leg as soon as the first fills is a different variant and is the planner's call, not added.

## 10. NO-BOUNCE CHECK (planner order 2026-11-14, user-approved; rules fixed 2026-11-18 before any code or result; analysis on existing data, local only, no new pull, no engine change)
QUESTION. Is the reversion real in book-midpoint terms, or was it the last-trade close bouncing between bid and ask? Events and picks stay exactly as saved: the SIGNAL is still built from closes. A signal built from mids is NOT tested and the report says so. Only the prices used to measure the entry stretch, the path and the exit change. Findings are reported, not acted on; nothing is tuned.

10.1 PRICE SOURCE AND TIME ALIGNMENT (the point the checker is asked to review first)
- PRIMARY mid = ctxs mid_px (the exchange's own mid; the cache used for the proxy in section 1). COVERAGE IS NOT FULL (checker I1): mid_px is 0 for whole coins on some days (17% of rows on 2026-01-15, 24% on 2026-10-05); on the vault taker open10 entries both mids are > 0 for 3,049 of 3,789 trades (80.5%) and from 2026-08-30 most days are under 50%. So coverage is printed, not assumed, and the matched set is not random (it leans to the earlier days). The engine maps a ctxs row stamped T to LABEL T-1 minute (clean_ctx_group: label = time - 1 min; off-minute stamps dropped). The engine's close of label L is the last trade before wall-clock L+1. So the mid that matches the close of label L is the ctxs row stamped L+1, i.e. the mid at the wall-clock moment the bar closes. Path index k (engine: label t0-1+k) therefore reads the ctxs row stamped t0+k. Entry reference (close of label t0-1) pairs with the row stamped t0; exit reference (close of label exit_time-1) pairs with the row stamped exit_time. The ctxs row is taken at the minute boundary plus a capture delay of a few seconds (G8 table snap_off_s: 0.3 s early periods, 2.7 s from 2026-09-15), so the mid is up to a few seconds later than the bar's last possible trade. That small drift is an ASSUMPTION, stated, not corrected.
- CROSS-CHECK: the G8 table mid (the first snapshot of the minute, computed from the book) at the same wall-clock minute stamp. On every (coin, minute) that both have (the g8_rows table), print the distribution of |ctxs mid - G8 mid| in bp (p50, p90, p99, max, share within 1 bp) by liquidity tier and period. It is a receipt: a tier with under 99% of rows within 1 bp is flagged in the print. The primary stays the ctxs mid; the G8 mid enters only as the labelled fallback below.
- G8 FALLBACK (planner ruling 2026-11-18), for the TRADE re-pricing of 10.5 only: a (coin, stamp) whose ctxs mid is missing or <= 0 takes the G8 table mid at the same stamp, labelled. Alignment: the G8 table row of minute m is the first snapshot of wall-clock minute m (0.3 s to 2.7 s after the boundary), the same instant as the ctxs row stamped m, so it pairs with the close of label m-1 exactly as the ctxs mid does (the table is keyed by wall-clock minute, as the section 8 fills are). The G8 table exists only for the entry and exit windows of the saved trades (and misses 7 days), so event-level tables (10.3, 10.4) rest on the ctxs mid only. Both versions of the trade table are printed: ctxs-only matched set (coverage and result) and ctxs-with-G8-fallback matched set. The 90% gate of 10.5 applies to the coverage WITH the fallback; if it still fails the headline is unbanded with the coverage stated. Coverage is printed by period, by month and by tier (events and trades).
- Prices enter as log(mid). A mid that is NaN or <= 0 is missing. No forward fill for the entry and exit prices.
- MISSING MIDS. A trade needs the mid of both legs at its entry stamp AND at its exit stamp; otherwise it is lost from the mid columns (counted). An event needs the mid of both legs at its entry stamp; otherwise it is lost from the mid columns (counted). Path statistics test minutes where both legs have a mid; a missing minute can only delay the first hit, and events with at least one missing path minute are counted. The horizon value (10.4) uses the minute exactly, or the last minute with both mids within 5 minutes before it, otherwise the event is dropped from that horizon (counted). The script prints, by period and by liquidity tier, the share of events and of trades lost for lack of a mid, and for trades by run.
- MISSING MINUTES BIAS THE REACH (found in a 3,000-event smoke run, rule added before the real run): a crossing inside a minute without a mid cannot be seen, so a mid reach computed on the available minutes is understated against a close reach computed on all minutes (35% of events have at least one missing mid minute; coverage falls over time). The matched comparator is therefore the CLOSE TESTED ONLY ON THE MINUTES WHERE THE MID EXISTS (both legs), and the horizon gross move of the close is read at the same minute as the mid's. The close on all minutes (and the saved labels) are printed beside as the unmatched reference. The 99.9% label receipt keeps using the close on all minutes.
- Close column on the SAME events: every comparison is matched, close and mid on exactly the same events or trades (those with all mids needed). Close figures on ALL events (saved labels) are printed beside as the unmatched reference.

10.2 SPREAD GEOMETRY AND SIGN. P_mid[k] = (logmid_a(k) - beta x logmid_b(k) - alpha - mu) / sigma with the event's own beta, alpha, mu, sigma from events.parquet (the engine's formula, compute_move_paths). s = +1 if z >= 0 else -1. Close-based P from the cleaned 1-minute closes the engine used (Prices class, costs_analysis.py). The spread has reached mu at minute k when s x P[k] <= 0 (same as the engine's move[k] >= |z|). Entry stretch in sigma: close |z| = s x P_close[0] (equals the saved |z|; asserted), mid |z_mid| = s x P_mid[0] (can be <= 0 when the stretch was all bounce). All distances are in sigma, not percent.

NAMING AND FRESHNESS (checker I2, planner ruling): the engine's close is the LAST TRADE, which can be old (cleaned prices: the share of minutes with a trade in that minute, mins_since_trade = 0, is 29% HMSTR, 38% YZY, 39% TNSR, 52% BOME, 83% LINEA, 96% AAVE). The measured quantity is therefore called the CLOSE VS MID GAP, never 'bounce' as such. A close is FRESH when mins_since_trade = 0 at its label (entry: label t0-1; exit: label exit_time-1). An event (trade) is FRESH when the closes of BOTH legs at the entry label (for a trade: at the entry and the exit labels, four closes) are fresh, STALE otherwise. Only the fresh stratum's gap may be called bounce; the stale stratum's gap is called stale price. Stretch (10.3) and the trade table (10.5) are stratified fresh / stale; the fresh-close share is printed by coin and by tier (share of the coin's minutes in the period with mins_since_trade = 0, and share of the events' entry legs).

10.3 ENTRY STRETCH (all events and the q 1% picks; vault and test months; by tier; and fresh / stale). Mean, median and quantiles (1, 5, 25, 50, 75, 95, 99) of |z|, |z_mid|, and d = |z| - |z_mid|. BOUNCE SHARE OF THE STRETCH = sum(d) / sum(|z|) over the matched set (and its median of d / |z| per event). Share of events with |z_mid| <= 0 (no stretch in mid terms) and with |z_mid| < |z| / 2. Picks = pick_0.01 of the picks table the engine used (vault: s1_vault_picks_all.parquet; test months: S1H.load_data + S1H.compute_picks, called not reimplemented, which carry raw p_10h and pick_0.01 for folds 1-7 and cross-check S1-G's saved per-fold n); assert that every event of the saved q 1% trades is a pick. "All events" = every event with t0 in the period that the trades' loaders return (vault: vault_loader.load_vault_events; test months: harness.load_search_events restricted to folds 1-7, the folds the saved trades cover; label_gap = 0 as the loaders return them).

10.4 REVERSION IN MID TERMS (all events and picks; horizons 2, 5, 10 h = 120, 300, 600 minutes; by tier and period)
- REACH RATE = share of events whose first minute k in 1..h with s x P[k] <= 0 exists; events already through mu at entry in mid terms (|z_mid| <= 0) are counted as NOT reached (the stretch was bounce) and listed in their own line; if the reader prefers them as reached, the line gives both. TIME TO MU = first such k, mean and median in minutes among the reached. MEAN GROSS MOVE in sigma at minute h (fixed horizon, no mu exit, no stop): -(P[h] - P[0]) x s, mid to mid and close to close, same events.
- Receipt: the close-based reach and time to mu recomputed by this script equal the saved labels (reach_2h/5h/10h and minutes_to_mu in labels.parquet, the engine's own source) on at least 99.9% of the events; any mismatch is listed and the run stops.
- RANKING IN MID TERMS: events of the period ranked by raw p_10h into quintiles within the period (rank method first; vault: s1_vault_picks_all.parquet; test months: the fold test predictions as S1-H loads them). 10-h reach rate by quintile, close and mid, matched; and LIFT AT THE TOP 1% = reach rate of the pick_0.01 events / reach rate of all events, close and mid.

10.5 RE-PRICING THE SAVED PRIORITY-RUN TRADES (q 1% / no stop; maker and taker; open10 and open20; both periods; the eight runs of section 7, exit time, exit reason and size exactly as saved; the exit decision itself rests on closes and is NOT re-decided)
- A = close-based zero-cost net = the saved net (receipt: sum equals the log's number).
- B = MID-TO-MID GROSS NET before any spread cost = saved net - saved gross + gross_mid, where gross_mid = la x dir_a x (exp(mv_a) - 1) + lb x dir_b x (exp(mv_b) - 1) with mv = log mid(exit stamp) - log mid(entry stamp) (the saved gross formula with mids; legs la, lb as saved at entry, the engine's convention); fees and funding exactly as saved (the run's own fee mode). So A and B differ only by the price series.
- C = costed net with the book half-spread, depth impact and fees as already modelled (the HEADLINE cell of section 7 and the per-trade headline files of costs_analysis.py). ## 11. ONE-LEG CHECK: is the profit mostly generated by one leg? (planner order 2026-11-18, user-approved; existing data only, no pull, no engine change; rules fixed before any code or result)
SCOPE. The eight headline runs (q 1% / no stop; maker and taker; open10 and open20; vault and test months), the saved trades, exit times, exit reasons, directions and leg sizes as saved. Findings are reported, not acted on; no parameter, pick or exit is changed.

11.1 LEG GROSS. For trade i, leg A = ticker_a, leg B = ticker_b; la = N/(1+|beta|), lb = N|beta|/(1+|beta|); dir_a = -1 if z >= 0 else +1; dir_b = (+1 if z >= 0 else -1) x sign(beta) (all as in RunData and the saved gross formula). Leg gross_a = la x dir_a x (exp(mv_a) - 1), leg gross_b = lb x dir_b x (exp(mv_b) - 1), mv = log price(exit stamp) - log price(entry stamp), stamps as in section 10.1 (the close of label t0-1 sits at stamp t0, the close of label exit_time-1 at stamp exit_time). CLOSE version: cleaned closes. MID version: ctxs mid with the G8 table mid as the labelled fallback (the version of the headline of section 10), on the trades with all four mids; coverage printed by period, month and tier as in section 10. ASSERT: gross_a + gross_b equals the saved gross_pnl for every trade (tolerance 1e-4); the maximum difference is printed. The mid version's trade total is compared with the section 10 gross_mid of the same run (printed, equal within 1e-6).
A pair with beta < 0 has two legs in the SAME direction (both long or both short); the share of such trades is printed, and the long/short split is reported twice (all legs; opposite-direction trades only).

11.2 SPLITS (each in close terms and, where the mids exist, in mid terms; all by period, by liquidity tier (the trade's least liquid leg, the costs1 tier mapping, which reproduces Part C's terciles: receipt of section 7), and by exit reason (exit reasons as saved: mu, timeout, and any other value found, each its own row))
(a) LONG vs SHORT: legs with dir = +1 vs dir = -1; per group: leg count, total gross, mean gross per leg, share of the run's saved gross.
(b) MOVER vs HEDGE (definitions fixed here; checker ruling 1 and planner ruling 2026-11-18): r60 of a leg = log close(label t0-1) - log close(label t0-61) from the cleaned closes (the 60 minutes before the entry). Two separate splits are printed. HEADLINE = BETA-ADJUSTED: the spread is ln a - beta ln b, so the MOVER is the leg whose beta-weighted 60-minute return moved the spread more: leg A if |r60_a| > |beta| x |r60_b|, leg B if |beta| x |r60_b| > |r60_a|; HEDGE = the other. Printed beside it: the RAW version (the leg with the larger |r60|). A trade with a missing r60 or a tie is UNCLASSIFIED (counted, kept in the totals of the other splits). Classification always uses closes, also in the mid version.
(c) LARGER vs SMALLER NOTIONAL LEG: la vs lb at entry; a trade with la = lb (to 1e-9) is counted as EQUAL and excluded from the split. This is the same split as |beta| < 1 versus |beta| > 1 (leg A is the larger leg when |beta| < 1, leg B when |beta| > 1), stated so the reader does not count it twice.
(d) ONE-LEG TRADES: among trades with saved gross > 0, the share where one leg's gross exceeds 100% of the trade's gross (the other leg lost money), and for those trades which leg it was (long/short, mover/hedge, larger/smaller). Among trades with saved gross < 0, the share where one leg made money. Also the share of the run's positive gross that comes from trades of the first kind. Also the leg-sign table per run (checker rulings 4 and 2026-11-18 zero-leg edit): 'up' = leg gross > 0, 'down' = < 0, 'flat' = exactly 0 (an illiquid leg with no trade between entry and exit has mv = 0), the six unordered pairs (up+up, down+down, flat+flat, up+down, up+flat, down+flat) with the trade count and the total gross of each cell, so 'one leg exceeds 100%' can be read: a hedged pair often has one leg losing. 'One leg exceeds 100% of the trade gross' means the other leg is strictly < 0; trades where the other leg is flat are counted on their own line ('one leg, other flat'), not as exceeding.

11.3 MARKET EXPOSURE. Net notional of trade i = la x dir_a + lb x dir_b (long minus short, $) and net share = net / N; print the mean, the mean of |net share| and quantiles 1/5/25/50/75/95/99. BTC return over the trade = log BTC(exit) - log BTC(entry). BTC is NOT among the coins of the cleaned 1-minute price set (checked: no BTC file in logs/profit1/cleaned or cleaned_vault), so the feather is kept (checker ruling 2): user_data/normalized/BTC.feather, 30-minute closes, as in Part C: BTC(x) = the close of the last 30-minute bar that ended at or before x, taking `date` as the bar's OPEN time (assumption, stated; the opposite convention, `date` = bar end, is printed as one labelled alternative line per run). Either way the price is up to 30 minutes old at both ends on trades that last minutes to hours; this blurs the slope toward zero and is stated. Slope and correlation are DESCRIPTIVE only: no p-values or intervals (the trades overlap in time). Per run: slope of the trade gross ($) on the BTC return (dollars per +1% BTC move) and Pearson correlation, the same for gross as bp of notional; mean gross by tercile of the BTC return of the run's trades; and the exposure P&L = sum of net$ x (exp(BTC return) - 1) (the P&L of the net exposure if both legs moved one-for-one with BTC; assumption, stated) next to the run's gross.

11.4 COSTS PER LEG. Per leg, fees as saved: the saved entry_fee and exit_fee of a trade split by leg in proportion to the leg's entry and exit USD (the rate is the same on both legs, so this is exact). For the four TAKER runs the half-spread is applied as well: leg cost = half-spread bp at the entry stamp x entry USD + half-spread bp at the exit stamp x exit USD, half-spread = G8 table spread_bp / 2 of the leg's coin in that wall-clock minute (own minute only; a trade with a missing or crossed row on either leg at either stamp is left out of the half-spread table and counted). Depth impact beyond the half-spread, funding (saved per trade, not per leg), and the maker fill model (limit fills, crossings, adverse selection) are NOT split by leg and are not in this table; the four MAKER runs show fees only and are labelled so. Leg net = leg gross - leg fees - leg half-spread cost. Printed by leg type (long/short, mover/hedge both versions, larger/smaller) and tier. LABEL on the table (planner ruling and checker ruling 3): 'leg net is NOT the costed net C; no depth impact, no funding, no fill model; this answers which leg makes the profit only'. RECEIPTS: the leg fees sum to the saved entry_fee + exit_fee on every trade (assert; the maximum difference is printed); per run, the sum of leg nets is printed next to the saved net A, and the gap is named (funding, plus the half-spread on taker runs).

Output: costs_legs.py, its log and json, a section file written from the json (every number script-printed, plain-language sentences are templates filled from the data); code and rules to the checker before the run; log and section before reporting; one short report to the planner.
