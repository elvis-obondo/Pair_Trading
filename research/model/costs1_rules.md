# COSTS1 decision rules (written before any L2 file is pulled or any cost is computed)

Task (planner, from the user): does the edge survive realistic execution costs? Analysis on the saved vault and test-month trades. No tuning of q, stops or timeout. Costs are reported, not optimised around.
Runs in scope (priority): q 1% / no stop / maker and taker, max_open 10 and 20, vault tags `vault_open10` / `vault_open20` and test months `base` / `open20`. The other 44 saved runs per side are NOT re-costed unless the planner asks (the L2 pull covers only the priority trades).

## 0. Conventions taken from the engine (s1_profit1.py, verified by reading it)
- Entry fill: wall-clock minute `t0` of the trade file; priced at the close of bar label `t0 - 1` (reference price `ref_in`). Exit fill: wall-clock `exit_time`, priced at the close of bar label `exit_time - 1` (`ref_out`).
- Legs: `leg_a = notional / (1 + |beta|)`, `leg_b = notional * |beta| / (1 + |beta|)`; `dir_a = -1 if z >= 0 else +1`, `dir_b = (1 if z >= 0 else -1) * (1 if beta >= 0 else -1)`; dir +1 = buy at entry, sell at exit. Exit leg USD = entry leg USD x exp(dlog price from entry to exit) from the cleaned 1-minute closes.
- Engine fees: every non-liquidation exit, including stops and timeouts, is charged the run's own fee mode (maker runs: 0.015%), entry too. Slippage is 0 in every saved run. Funding as saved.
- Zero-cost reference per trade: the saved `net` (gross - fees - funding, slippage 0).

## 1. Books
- Entry book for a leg = the snapshot with the minimum exchange time inside UTC minute `t0` (extractor build9_l2_books.py); exit book = same for minute `exit_time`. Books at the minutes after (up to +15) serve the waiting windows and the taker fallback.
- If the wanted minute has no snapshot in the pulled files: use the nearest wanted minute within +-1; else the "no book" proxy (below). The count of trade-legs by source (own minute / +-1 / proxy) is printed per period and tier.
- Beyond-book: if the 20 levels hold less USD than the leg, the stored levels are walked at their prices and only the REMAINDER is priced at the last stored level (an ESTIMATE (not a bound in either direction, checker 2026-11-14)) and the leg is flagged `beyond_book`; (RESOLUTION 2026-11-14, planner: sections 1 and 8 disagreed; section 1's wording is the HEADLINE, with the table the "stored levels" are the CURVE points: the first D_last USD at the curve's average cost, the remainder at the last stored level. Section 8's wording "the leg is priced at book_reach_bp", i.e. the WHOLE leg at the last level, is printed beside it, with the net without beyond-book trades, for both periods. The vault letter is unaffected; test-month numbers change and both versions are shown.) the share of legs flagged is printed, and totals are also printed with those trades excluded.
- No-book proxy (labelled, weak): half the ctxs impact spread, 0.5 x (impact_ask - impact_bid) / mid, per leg, from the saved ctxs cache at that minute. It is measured at the exchange's fixed impact notional, not at our size, so it overstates the cost of small legs; it is a ceiling-like proxy, not a measurement.

## 2. Taker fill (entry and exit)
- Cost = walk the opposite side of the book with the leg's USD (price x size, cumulative); average price `p_fill`. Buy: cost_bp = (p_fill / mid - 1) x 1e4; sell: (1 - p_fill / mid) x 1e4, `mid` = (best bid + best ask) / 2 of that book. Taker fee 0.045% on the leg USD.
- Exact accounting against the engine's reference price, per leg: entry gain = dir x leg_usd x (ref_in - p_fill_in) / ref_in; exit gain = dir x leg_usd_out x (p_fill_out - ref_out) / ref_out. net_new = saved gross + sum of gains - new fees - saved funding. The decomposition vs book mid (half spread, walk) is printed next to it; (ref - mid) should average about zero (printed as a check).

## 3. Maker fill (maker runs only; both legs attempt maker; taker runs are all-taker)
- Entry limit: buy leg at the best bid, sell leg at the best ask of the entry book. Waiting window W minutes starting at `t0` (bars labelled t0 .. t0 + W - 1 from the build2 1-minute cache). Primary W = 5; sensitivity W = 1 and 15.
- LENIENT fill: first bar with low <= limit (buy) / high >= limit (sell). STRICT fill: first bar with low <= limit - tick (buy) / high >= limit + tick (sell), where tick = the smallest positive price step between adjacent levels in the entry book (an estimate; labelled). The two are the range; no queue position is observed (no tick data), so no point estimate is claimed between them.
- Fill price = the limit; fee maker 0.015%. A leg filled at bar f has fill time f (minute start; sub-minute order unknown).
- Both legs filled within W -> trade kept (entered at f_late; the exit is the saved exit, so a leg filled at f >= exit minute makes the trade MISSED). Exactly one leg unfilled at the end of W -> that leg is crossed as taker at the book of minute t0 + W (walk as in 2; the drift to that minute is inside the accounting vs ref_in), the trade is kept. Both legs unfilled -> trade MISSED (net 0, no cost; the slot it occupied in the saved run is not refilled).
- Exit, exit reason mu in a maker run: limit at the best passive price of the exit book, window W from `exit_time` with the same lenient / strict test on bars exit_time .. exit_time + W - 1; unfilled at W -> taker at the book of minute exit_time + W (price drift accounted vs ref_out). Exit reason stop or timeout, and every exit in taker runs: taker at the exit-minute book. The saved maker-run exits for stops/timeouts used the maker fee: its effect is reported as its own line (net of the existing maker numbers with those exits' fees raised to taker, nothing else changed; no run is edited).
- Fill rate = share of legs and share of trades filled, overall, by period and by liquidity tier. Adverse selection: for missed vs filled trades (each rule) compare the saved labels `reach_10h`, `minutes_to_mu`, and the saved gross in sigma (means, n, standard errors; no test statistics beyond that).

## 4. Two-leg risk
- For trades where the legs fill at different bars: gap in minutes, share of trades, and the unhedged leg's log-price move (bp, signed to our position) between its fill and the other leg's fill (or W), from the cleaned closes; distribution (mean, p50, p90, worst) and its total PnL effect (already inside the net via the exact accounting).

## 5. Re-costed results
- Per run and period: trades filled, net PnL, profit factor (sum of positive nets / sum of negative nets), Sharpe (daily realised PnL by exit day, mean / std x sqrt(365)), max drawdown (daily realised equity from $1,000, sizing held as saved), shown next to the SAME method on the zero-cost saved trades and next to the engine's own reported numbers. Equity is rebuilt from per-trade nets in exit-time order; compounding/sizing and slot refilling are NOT re-fed (stated).
- Same by coin (a trade counts for both legs, as in Part C), by pair, and by liquidity tier (terciles of each coin's median daily traded notional, build2 1-minute cache, as Part C); a trade takes its less liquid leg's tier. Question to answer from it: is the least liquid third's share of net (82.9% vault maker open10) real after costs, or flattering?
- Break-even uniform extra slippage per fill under this model vs the arithmetic of Part C (vault q 1% no stop maker open10: 5.43 bp; taker 1.77 bp).
- Sensitivity as a RANGE: W (1, 5, 15) x fill rule (lenient, strict) x impact multiplier on the walk and proxy cost (0.5, 1, 2). The impact term is an estimate, labelled.

## 6. Yardsticks and questions
- (a) Volume vs depth: fit walk cost (net of half-spread) on sqrt(leg / minute traded notional) over legs with a book; report slope, R^2 and the rank correlation, by tier; the fitted line is used only as a labelled estimate where a book has no depth for the leg (beyond_book), never to overwrite a measured cost.
- (b) Leverage caps: trades and net by leverage value (share on 3x, 5x, 10x...); what changes if a cap differs is stated as sizing (notional) not signals; historical caps are not available and nothing is simulated.
- Entry delay (1, 5, 15 min): write-up only, no engine change, no run.

## 7. Headline, answer rule and reporting conventions (checker review 2026-11-13, R1-R6, fixed before the analysis script exists)
- R1. HEADLINE CELL, fixed now: maker runs = strict fill rule, W = 5, impact multiplier 1, beyond-book legs at the last level (lower bound); taker runs = impact multiplier 1. The answer sentence is generated by the script from the sensitivity grid: "net positive in k of N sensitivity cells; headline net X (vault) and Y (test months)", per configuration, with no pass/fail wording. A day-block bootstrap interval on the headline net (as in S1-D) is suggested to the planner; it is not added without their call.
- R2. A bar that is missing or NaN inside a fill window counts as no fill, and the number of such legs is printed.
- R3. Beyond-book legs: the HEADLINE uses the last stored level (section 1). The fitted line of 6a is shown only as a separate, labelled column; it never enters the headline.
- R4. Profit factor, Sharpe and drawdown are from daily REALISED PnL by exit day. The engine's own are from marked account value; they are shown as reference only. The like-for-like baseline is the same method on the zero-cost saved trades.
- R5. The first snapshot of a minute is about 0.3 s in (pilot, early periods) and about 2.7 s in (2026-09-15) while the bar low/high covers the whole minute, so a lenient fill in the first bar can use trades from before the order existed: lenient is an upper bound on the fill rate. Print snap_off_s by period.
- R6. The headline table carries the missed-trade share next to net per FILLED trade, and net per trade of the SAVED set (missed counted as 0), so a rising net per filled trade cannot hide a falling fill rate. Missed shares are printed by cause (both legs unfilled; a leg filled at or after the exit minute) per period and run.
- Print (ref - mid) by liquidity tier (the engine's reference is a last-trade close and carries bid-ask bounce).
- Keep-and-cross at the end of W is the primary one-leg rule. Crossing the second leg as soon as the first fills is a different variant and is the planner's call, not added.

## 8. G8 table in place of the raw books (planner ruling 2026-11-13); validation; escalation; verdict (fixed before any result)
Source for the headline: s3://pair-trading-hl-289869684922/build8/full/{day}_l2.parquet (G8 raw pass, first snapshot of every coin-minute: spread_bp, mid, bid_d10/25/50, ask_d10/25/50, trunc flags, slip_buy_1k, slip_sell_1k, book_reach_bid/ask_bp, crossed, snap_off_s). The rows needed (every minute of the entry window [t0-1, t0+15] and exit window [exit-1, exit+15] for both legs of the priority trades) are extracted on an EC2 in Tokyo. Sections 1-3 apply with "book" read as "table row at that minute", and the walk replaced by the CURVE below. The raw-book extractor (build9_l2_books.py) is kept for validation and fallback.
- CURVE (a CONSERVATIVE bound, stated as such): for a leg of USD S on the side being hit (buy: ask side, sell: bid side), cost_bp(S) = piecewise-linear interpolation through the points (0, spread_bp/2), (1000, slip_1k), (D10, 10), (D25, 25), (D50, 50), sorted by USD, with D_e the side's cumulative USD within e bp; points with NaN or non-increasing USD are dropped, and the cost is made non-decreasing in S. For S < 1000 with a finite slip_1k this is the straight line from half-spread to slip_1k, which OVERSTATES the cost of a convex book walk (a real walk is cheaper at small S). Each depth point (D_e, e) is an upper bound of the average cost at that size (the average fill price lies inside the band). Beyond the last point (S > D50, or a book with fewer than $1,000 inside the 20 levels and no band point) the leg is flagged beyond_book. HEADLINE (resolution 2026-11-14, see section 1): the first D_last USD (D_last = USD of the last curve point) cost the curve's value there, the REMAINDER S - D_last is priced at max(last curve cost, book_reach_bp), so the leg's average cost is (D_last x c_last + (S - D_last) x reach) / S, an estimate (not a bound). The earlier wording of this section (the WHOLE leg at book_reach_bp) is printed beside it as "section 8 as written". Rows with crossed = 1 or no row: no-book proxy (section 1).

## 9. What this does NOT do
No tuning of q, stops, timeout, or any cut-off; no re-selection of picks; no engine change; no tick data; no queue model beyond the lenient/strict pair; no refill of freed slots; no compounding re-feed.
