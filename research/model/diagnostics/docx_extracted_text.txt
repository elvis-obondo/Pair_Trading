Runs ending above the $1,000 start: 18/24.
Key findings
Of the 24 base (max_open=10) configurations, 18/24 end the 7 test blocks (2025-12-21 to 2026-07-12) above the $1,000 start.
The best base config is q=0.01, stop=none, fee=maker (total_return=4.0344); the worst is q=0.01, stop=0p5, fee=taker (total_return=-0.8444).
EV quintiles (stop=none only) rank realised net monotonically (quintile 0 to 4 non-decreasing) in 0/6 of the base q x fee combinations -- EV here is descriptive only and does not rank realised net in these tables.
Raising max_open from 10 to 20 (Task A) moves the count of runs ending above start from 18/24 (max_open=10) to 19/24 (max_open=20). margin skips are 0 in 24/24 base (max_open=10) runs, and range from 30 to 10,140 across the 24 open20 (max_open=20) runs: at max_open=10 the open positions can need at most 50% of account value as margin (10 x 5%), so margin cannot bind; at max_open=20 that rises to 100% (20 x 5%), so it can.
Task B's descriptive |z| x p_cal grid on the 6 stop=none runs finds the top p_cal quintile has the highest mean return in 6/6 runs, but win_rate is monotonic in only 2/6 runs for |z| and 2/6 for p_cal, and mean return is monotonic in 0/6 runs for |z| and 0/6 for p_cal.
Task C's |z| >= 3.0 filter (in-sample -- chosen after seeing these test months) cuts the q=0.01 pick count from 35,629 to 4,063, q=0.005 from 17,699 to 2,046, and q=0.001 from 3,638 to 434, before any skip is applied (identical in both z3_open10 and z3_open20).
Known gaps
slippage excluded by the user's choice (legs are small); costs are a lower bound and results an upper bound (optimistic).
max_leverage is today's snapshot (hindsight); limits were different in the past.
liquidation is checked on 1-minute closes; a move inside a minute can liquidate earlier.
maker fills are assumed to fill; in practice they may not.
the test months were already used for the cut-off, the deep dive and the calibration: results are optimistic until the vault or paper-trading check.
2. Glossary
term
meaning
q
pick threshold -- the fraction of events taken as a trade (1% is the loosest, 0.1% the tightest).
stop
a stop-loss in standard deviations (sigma) of the pair spread; 'none' means no stop, hold to timeout.
z
the spread's distance from its mean at entry, in sigma units -- the signal S1 scores.
p_cal_10h
S1-H's calibrated probability the spread reverts to mean within 10 hours.
final_equity
account value in USD at the end of the 7-month test period, starting from $1,000.
total_return
final_equity / 1000 - 1.
max_open
the maximum number of concurrent open positions allowed.
margin skip
a pick that was not taken because taking it would exceed the account's margin.
profit_factor
sum of winning trades' net $ divided by the absolute sum of losing trades' net $.
distance to liquidation (ratio)
account_value / maintenance margin; 1.0 would mean liquidation.
in-sample
a result where the rule or threshold was chosen after seeing these same test months -- not yet validated out of sample.
